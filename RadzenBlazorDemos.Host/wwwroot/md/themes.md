### Free Themes

These themes are free for commercial use. The source SCSS files are licensed under MIT and available on [GitHub](https://github.com/radzenhq/radzen-blazor).

### Premium Themes PRO

Premium themes can be used with an active [Radzen Blazor subscription](https://github.com/radzenhq/radzen-blazor).

### Swatches PRO

Swatches are a premium feature of Radzen Blazor Studio. Each theme contains a collection of predefined color swatches to pick from.
