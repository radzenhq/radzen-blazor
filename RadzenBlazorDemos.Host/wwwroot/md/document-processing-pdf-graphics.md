# Images & Graphics

Add images, gradients, shadows, opacity, and blend modes to PDF documents in Blazor and C#. Overlay content, apply watermarks, and render barcodes and QR codes.

Keywords: document, processing, pdf, image, png, jpeg, gradient, shadow, opacity, blend, overlay, watermark, barcode, qr, qrcode

## Examples

## PDF Images & Graphics

Place images, decorate content with gradients, shadows, and blend modes, overlay elements, watermark pages, and render barcodes and QR codes directly into the document flow.

### Images

Add PNG, JPEG, and JPEG 2000 images as blocks or inline with text. PNG transparency is preserved, and images scale to a fit box or to explicit dimensions.

### Gradients & effects

Style containers with linear and radial gradients, rounded corners, box shadows, opacity, rotation, and sixteen blend modes.

### Z-order

Overlay containers stack content in a deterministic paint order - badges over images, text over shapes.

### Watermarks

Stamp every page of a section with rotated text or an image at configurable opacity.

### Barcodes & QR codes

Render barcodes and QR codes as vector content in the document flow with `AddBarcode` and `AddQrCode`, including QR error-correction levels and eye and module shapes.
