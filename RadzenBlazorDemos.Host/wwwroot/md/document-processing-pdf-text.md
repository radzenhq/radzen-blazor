## Examples

## PDF Text & Layout

Format text with named styles, control how paragraphs break across pages, and add running headers and footers with page numbers.

### Styles

Define named styles with inheritance and apply them to paragraphs and runs. Character formatting covers font family, size, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, color, letter spacing, and superscript and subscript.

### Pagination

Control page breaking with widow and orphan limits, `KeepTogether`, `KeepWithNext`, and explicit page breaks. Adjust the settings and watch content move between pages.

### Headers & footers

Add running headers and footers to a section, including page numbers and page counts rendered with `PageNumberField` and `PageCountField`.

### Tab stops

Align content with left, center, right, and decimal tab stops, with optional dot leaders.
